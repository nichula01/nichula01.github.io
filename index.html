About

About

Nichula Wasalathilaka is an AI researcher and final-year Electrical and Electronic Engineering undergraduate at the University of Peradeniya. He is also a visiting research student at Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI).

His work focuses on AI-driven methods for remote sensing, medical image analysis, and intelligent engineering systems, with an emphasis on robust machine learning that connects technical rigor to real-world applications.
